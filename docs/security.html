LIQUIDITY SECURITY

Liquidity Lock & Proof

Liquidity security is separate from contract-code security. VIKASA will publish verifiable liquidity-lock information when liquidity is locked, including the relevant lock proof and on-chain details. No current liquidity-lock claim is made here unless it is independently verifiable on-chain.

How We Approach Risk

The practical decisions behind the roadmap above.

🔒

Fixed 24M supply

VIK has a maximum supply of 24,000,000 tokens on Polygon PoS. The deployed token contract does not provide a mint function for increasing supply beyond the fixed cap.

🧪

Two automated assessments

VIK has published automated security assessments from Audit Forge and Quantum Audit. They evaluate different aspects and should be read together.

🔍

Public verification

The contract source and on-chain token data can be independently checked on PolygonScan. Users do not need to rely only on statements made on this website.

⚠️

Risk disclosures

Automated assessments do not eliminate risk. Liquidity, holder concentration, administrative control and market conditions can change after an assessment is published.

💧

Liquidity transparency

Liquidity security is tracked separately from contract-code security. Any future liquidity lock will be supported by publicly verifiable on-chain proof.

📖

Public documentation

Technical documentation is published openly on GitHub, alongside the project's whitepaper and on-chain references.

⚠️ Important security disclosure

Audit Forge and Quantum Audit are automated security-assessment platforms. Their reports are useful for transparency and risk identification, but they are not professional manual smart-contract audits and do not guarantee that the contract or ecosystem is free from vulnerabilities. Quantum's risk score is a snapshot of on-chain conditions and can change as liquidity, holders and market activity change. VIK is a utility reward token and does not represent equity ownership or a guaranteed financial return. Always verify contract, liquidity and wallet data independently before making decisions.
