## Important Audit Disclosure

The above assessments are **automated security assessments and are not professional manual smart-contract audits**.

Automated security assessments cannot guarantee that a smart contract or the wider VIKASA ecosystem is completely free from vulnerabilities.

They also do not guarantee:

* Complete smart-contract security
* Protection against private-key compromise
* Protection against operational or infrastructure risks
* Liquidity security
* Market stability
* Future code changes
* Security of future integrations

A professional manual third-party smart-contract audit remains a future security milestone as the VIKASA ecosystem grows and additional functionality is introduced.
